Below are my personal feedback and overall evaluation regarding my internship experience in the **AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program:

---

### OVERALL EVALUATION

#### 1. Working Environment
The working environment at First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) is dynamic, friendly, and open. FCAJ team members are always willing to support and answer questions whenever I encounter challenges while adapting to new Cloud & AI technologies, even outside working hours. The modern, well-organized workspace equipped with necessary tools allows high focus during project deployment. However, I think the program could organize a few more team bonding activities between intern groups to foster better connections.

#### 2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin
Mentors are extremely dedicated and thorough, always explaining technical fundamentals clearly when complex issues arise (such as IAM OIDC, CloudFront OAC, or AWS CI/CD pipelines). Rather than just giving solutions, mentors encourage independent research, experimentation, and troubleshooting before offering guidance, building self-reliance. The admin team closely supports administrative procedures, AWS resource access, and ensures optimal conditions for task completion.

#### 3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major
Assigned project tasks perfectly match my Information Technology / Cloud Computing major. The work reinforces theoretical knowledge learned at university while introducing advanced real-world cloud practices.

#### 4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities
Through FCAJ, I gained valuable practical skills: mastering AWS core services (EC2, S3, ECR, CloudFront, CloudWatch, Bedrock), standard DevOps workflows, Git/Monorepo code management, and technical documentation writing. Beyond hard skills, mentor insights improved my teamwork, time management, and Cloud & AI career path planning.

#### 5. Company Culture & Team Spirit
FCAJ culture is highly positive, prioritizing respect, transparency, and mutual support. Everyone works responsibly in an enjoyable atmosphere. During critical project phases or infrastructure incidents, members collaborate without barriers between interns and full-time engineers, making me feel genuinely part of the team.

#### 6. Internship Allowance & Benefits
The program offers reasonable internship allowances and flexible schedule management when academic classes require attendance. Having full access to AWS hands-on resources and participating in internal deep-dive sharing sessions adds substantial practical value.

---

### ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS

- **Most Satisfying Experience:** Being trusted to work directly on real-world Cloud & AI stacks. Successfully deploying a production-grade web application onto AWS and mastering automated CI/CD pipelines significantly accelerated my professional growth.
- **Areas for Improvement for Future Interns:** The program could introduce onboarding workshops covering general project architecture during the first 1–2 weeks to help future interns adapt faster.
- **Recommending to Friends:** I will definitely recommend FCAJ to my peers. It is an ideal internship environment for students passionate about Cloud, AI, and DevOps, offering real project exposure, expert mentorship, and professional culture.

---

### SUGGESTIONS & EXPECTATIONS

- **Experience Improvement Suggestions:**
  - Schedule periodic cross-team Code Review or Architecture Review sessions to learn optimization patterns from other projects.
  - Increase short social activities (Internal Coffee Talks) to strengthen team bonding.
- **Future Career Aspirations:** I strongly aspire to continue contributing and growing into a full-time position at FCAJ after completing my university graduation.
- **Acknowledgements & Additional Sharing:** I express my sincere gratitude to the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Management Board, Team Admins, and especially my Mentors for their guidance, mentorship, and constant encouragement throughout this internship. I wish FCAJ continued growth and success in cultivating future Cloud & AI engineering talents!
